02 — Outcomes

What you will learn

By the end of week 4 you will be able to hold basic conversations, read short texts, and write simple sentences in German.

🔤

Vocabulary (~300 words)

  • Numbers 1–100, dates & times
  • Days, months, seasons
  • Family & people
  • Food, drink & shopping
  • Home & classroom objects
  • Colors, clothes, weather
  • Common verbs (haben, sein, gehen…)
✏️

Grammar

  • German alphabet & pronunciation
  • Personal pronouns (ich, du, er…)
  • Present tense — regular & irregular
  • Nominative & accusative case
  • Definite & indefinite articles
  • Basic negation (nicht, kein)
  • Modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen)
🗣️

Speaking & listening

  • Introduce yourself & others
  • Ask & answer basic questions
  • Simple conversations (café, school)
  • Understand slow, clear German speech
  • Handle greetings & farewells
  • Tell the time and date
📄

Reading & writing

  • Read short texts (postcards, forms)
  • Write simple sentences
  • Fill in basic forms
  • Understand short instructions
  • Write a short personal profile
03 — Progress

How you will be assessed

Three friendly check-ins over the four weeks — nothing to stress about, just a chance to see how far you have come.

Check-in
Week 2 — start of session 3
A short, friendly quiz on what you have covered so far — alphabet, greetings, numbers and basic pronouns. Nothing to stress about, it just helps us see how you are getting on.
Mid-course task
Week 3 — session 5
You will introduce yourself and talk about your family in 5–6 sentences, in both speaking and writing. A great chance to see how much you already know!
Final mock test
Week 4 — session 8
A full practice run of the Goethe-Institut A1 exam — reading, listening, writing, and speaking. You will receive personal written feedback to celebrate your progress.

The mock test follows the official Goethe-Institut Fit in Deutsch 1 (A1) format. You can explore free practice materials on the Goethe-Institut website whenever you feel ready.
